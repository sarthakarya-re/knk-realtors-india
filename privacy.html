1. Introduction

KNK Realtors India ("KNK", "we", "us", "our") is a real estate brokerage operating in India's National Capital Region (NCR), representing developer partners including Trehan IRIS, Civitech and Saraswati Developers. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect personal data submitted through knkrealtorsindia.com (the "Site"), in accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act") and other applicable Indian law.

By submitting your details through our contact form, WhatsApp, email or phone, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this policy.

2. Data We Collect

We collect personal data only when you voluntarily provide it — for example, through our enquiry form or by contacting us directly. This may include:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • The project(s) or developer(s) you're interested in
  • Any message, budget range, or preferences you share with us
  • Records of your communications with our team (calls, WhatsApp, email)
  • Basic technical data such as browser type and general location, gathered automatically when you visit the Site

We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal data such as financial account numbers, government ID numbers, or health information through the Site.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and share relevant listing information
  • Arrange site visits, calls, or meetings with our advisors
  • Coordinate with the relevant developer partner on your behalf, where you've expressed interest in a specific project
  • Send you updates on projects you've enquired about, such as new inventory, price revisions, or possession timelines
  • Improve our Site and services
  • Meet legal, regulatory or RERA-related record-keeping obligations

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Legal Basis & Consent

We process your data on the basis of the consent you provide when submitting the enquiry form or initiating contact with us. You may withdraw this consent at any time by contacting us using the details in Section 13 — withdrawal will not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal, and may limit our ability to assist you with an ongoing enquiry.

5. Sharing With Developer Partners

Where you express interest in a specific project, we may share your name and contact details with the relevant developer partner (Trehan IRIS, Civitech, or Saraswati Developers) solely to facilitate your enquiry — for example, site visit scheduling or unit availability checks. We do not share your data with unrelated third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.

We may also share data with service providers who support our operations (such as hosting or communication tools) strictly to the extent needed to provide those services, and under obligations to protect your data.

We may disclose data where required by law, court order, or a valid request from a government or regulatory authority.

7. Security Measures

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including restricted internal access to enquiry data and secure transmission of data submitted through the Site. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

Right to Access

Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how it's being processed.

Right to Correction

Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your data once it's no longer needed for the purpose it was collected.

Right to Withdraw Consent

Withdraw consent for further processing at any time.

Right to Grievance Redressal

Raise a complaint about how your data has been handled.

Right to Nominate

Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 13. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe as required under the DPDP Act.

9. Cookies & Tracking

The Site does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. It may use minimal, functional local storage in your browser purely to support Site features (such as remembering filter selections during your visit). This data stays in your browser and is not transmitted to us.

10. Children's Data

Our services are intended for individuals aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If you believe a minor has submitted data to us, please contact us so we can delete it.

11. Grievance Redressal

If you have concerns about how your personal data has been handled, you may reach out to our Grievance Officer using the contact details below. We aim to acknowledge grievances promptly and resolve them within the timelines prescribed under the DPDP Act, 2023.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision. Continued use of the Site after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
